🧠 Building Your Innovation Idea

Although your assessment is an individual report, you are recommended to work in teams — so you can share ideas, receive feedback, and work professionally, collaboratively, and confidently.

Learning route: explore a problem, generate alternatives, test ideas through peer dialogue, then justify a decision. Focus on evidence and reasoning rather than trying to produce a perfect idea immediately.

This activity mirrors how innovation projects launch in real organisations. Take it seriously — what you establish now will save time, reduce stress, and improve the quality of your final submission.

The Power of Collaborative Innovation

Innovation is rarely a solo endeavor. The most groundbreaking ideas emerge when diverse perspectives collide, challenge assumptions, and build upon one another. Working together in an innovation team isn't just about dividing tasks—it's about multiplying creativity, mitigating blind spots, and transforming individual sparks into collective breakthroughs. Embrace the dialogue, value every voice, and remember: the best ideas are built together.
